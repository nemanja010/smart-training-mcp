## Exercise Selection Principles

### Stimulus-to-Fatigue Ratio (SFR)

**High SFR (prioritize):**
- Machine/cable exercises with good resistance curves
- Exercises at muscle's stretched position
- Stable positions allowing failure without injury risk

**Low SFR (use strategically):**
- Heavy deadlifts (high systemic fatigue)
- Barbell rows (spinal erector fatigue)
- Exercises requiring excessive stabilization

## Hamstring Exercises

### Knee Flexion (Shortening)

| Exercise | SFR | Notes |
|----------|-----|-------|
| **Seated Leg Curl** | High | BEST for hypertrophy (stretch) |

## Calf Exercises

### Gastrocnemius (Upper Calf)

| Exercise | SFR | Notes |
|----------|-----|-------|
| Standing Calf Raise | High | Straight legs |
| Donkey Calf Raise | High | Classic |
| Single-Leg Calf Raise | Moderate | Unilateral |

### Soleus (Lower Calf)

| Exercise | SFR | Notes |
|----------|-----|-------|
| Seated Calf Raise | High | Bent knee |
| Leg Press Calf Raise | Moderate | Alternative |

### Key Research Finding
**Kassiano et al. (2023):** Lengthened partials showed ~2x hypertrophy compared to full ROM for calves

### Training Protocol
- Deep stretch at bottom (2-3 sec hold)
- Full contraction at top
- Include both straight-leg and bent-knee variations
- High frequency (3-4x/week) often needed

## Exercise Substitutions

### When Exercise Causes Pain

| Original | Substitution |
|----------|--------------|
| Barbell Bench | DB Bench, Floor Press, Push-ups |
| Back Squat | Leg Press, Goblet Squat, Split Squat |
| Deadlift | Trap Bar DL, RDL, Leg Curl + Back Extension |
| Overhead Press | Landmine Press, High Incline, Lateral Raise |
| Barbell Row | Chest-Supported Row, Cable Row, DB Row |
| Skull Crushers | Pushdowns, Overhead Extension |

### Equipment Alternatives

| If You Don't Have | Use Instead |
|-------------------|-------------|
| Cables | Bands, free weights with pause |
| Leg Extension | Sissy Squat, Spanish Squat |
| Leg Curl | Nordic Curl, Sliding Leg Curl |
| Lat Pulldown | Pull-ups, Band Pulldowns |
| Pec Deck | DB Fly, Floor Fly |
