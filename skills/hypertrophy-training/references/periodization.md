## Periodization Models Overview

| Model | Description | Best For |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| **Linear (LP)** | Progressive intensity increase, volume decrease | Beginners |
| **DUP** | Daily varying intensity/volume | Intermediate+ |
| **Block** | Focused 3-6 week phases | Advanced |
| **Conjugate** | Concurrent qualities + variation | Powerlifters |
| **Autoregulation** | RPE/RIR-based adjustments | All levels |

---

## Linear Periodization

### Classic Structure

```
Phase 1 (Hypertrophy): 4-6 weeks
- Volume: High (4-5 sets × 8-12 reps)
- Intensity: 65-75% 1RM
- RIR: 3-4

Phase 2 (Strength): 3-4 weeks
- Volume: Moderate (4 sets × 4-6 reps)
- Intensity: 80-88% 1RM
- RIR: 2-3

Phase 3 (Peaking): 2-3 weeks
- Volume: Low (3-4 sets × 1-3 reps)
- Intensity: 90-100% 1RM
- RIR: 0-1

Deload: 1 week
```

### When to Use
- Beginners (first 1-2 years)
- After long layoff
- Simple, easy to follow
- When peaking for competition

### Limitations
- Long gaps between quality repetitions
- Detraining of other qualities during phases
- May plateau faster for advanced lifters

## Daily Undulating Periodization (DUP)

### Research Evidence

**Meta-analysis findings (Grgic et al. 2017; Iversen et al. 2022):**
- DUP slightly superior for strength (ES = 0.24-0.31)
- Similar hypertrophy outcomes to LP when volume equated
- Better retention of multiple qualities
- More variety, potentially better adherence

### Sample 4-Day DUP

| Day | Focus | Sets × Reps | Intensity |
|-----|-------|-------------|-----------|
| Monday | Strength | 5 × 3-5 | 85-90% |
| Tuesday | Hypertrophy | 4 × 8-12 | 70-75% |
| Thursday | Power | 5 × 2-3 | 80-85% |
| Friday | Volume | 3 × 12-15 | 65-70% |

### Weekly DUP Example (Squat)

| Week | Mon | Wed | Fri |
|------|-----|-----|-----|
| 1 | 4×5 @80% | 3×10 @70% | 5×3 @85% |
| 2 | 4×5 @82.5% | 3×10 @72.5% | 5×3 @87.5% |
| 3 | 4×5 @85% | 3×10 @75% | 5×3 @90% |
| 4 | Deload | | |

## Block Periodization

### Phase Structure

**Accumulation Block (3-4 weeks)**
- Goal: Build work capacity, hypertrophy
- Volume: High
- Intensity: Moderate (60-75%)
- Sets: 3-5 × 8-15 reps

**Transmutation Block (3-4 weeks)**
- Goal: Convert volume to strength
- Volume: Moderate
- Intensity: High (75-88%)
- Sets: 4-6 × 3-6 reps

**Realization Block (1-3 weeks)**
- Goal: Peak performance, express strength
- Volume: Low
- Intensity: Very high (88-100%)
- Sets: 3-5 × 1-3 reps

### Hypertrophy-Focused Block Structure

```
Block 1: Volume Accumulation (4-6 weeks)
├── Week 1: 10 sets/muscle, RIR 4
├── Week 2: 12 sets/muscle, RIR 3
├── Week 3: 14 sets/muscle, RIR 2
├── Week 4: 16 sets/muscle, RIR 2
├── Week 5: 18 sets/muscle, RIR 1
└── Week 6: Deload (9 sets, RIR 5)

Block 2: Intensification (3-4 weeks)
├── Week 1: 12 sets/muscle, heavier loads
├── Week 2: 14 sets/muscle
├── Week 3: 12 sets/muscle, test strength
└── Week 4: Deload

Block 3: Specialization (4 weeks)
├── Focus on weak points
├── Reduce volume on strong points
└── Advanced techniques
```

## Autoregulation Methods

### RPE Scale (Rate of Perceived Exertion)

| RPE | Description | RIR Equivalent |
|-----|-------------|----------------|
| 10 | Maximum effort, couldn't do more | 0 |
| 9.5 | Could maybe do 1 more | 0-1 |
| 9 | Could definitely do 1 more | 1 |
| 8.5 | Could do 1-2 more | 1-2 |
| 8 | Could do 2 more | 2 |
| 7.5 | Could do 2-3 more | 2-3 |
| 7 | Could do 3 more | 3 |
| 6 | Could do 4+ more | 4+ |

### RIR-Based Programming

**Week-to-week progression:**
```
Week 1: All sets RIR 4
Week 2: All sets RIR 3
Week 3: All sets RIR 2
Week 4: Last sets RIR 1
Week 5: Deload (RIR 5-6)
```

### Autoregulated Volume

**Method:** Adjust sets based on performance

| Scenario | Adjustment |
|----------|------------|
| Hitting top of rep range easily | Add 1 set next week |
| Struggling to hit minimum reps | Reduce 1 set |
| Excessive soreness >48h | Reduce volume 20% |
| Performance dropping | Take deload |

## Mesocycle Design

### Standard Hypertrophy Mesocycle (5-6 weeks)

```
Week 1: Introduction
├── Volume: MEV (minimum effective)
├── RIR: 4
├── Purpose: Establish baseline

Week 2: Accumulation
├── Volume: MEV + 10-20%
├── RIR: 3
├── Purpose: Build stimulus

Week 3: Accumulation
├── Volume: Approaching MAV
├── RIR: 2-3
├── Purpose: Push adaptation

Week 4: Intensification
├── Volume: MAV (peak)
├── RIR: 1-2
├── Purpose: Maximum stimulus

Week 5: Overreach (optional)
├── Volume: MRV
├── RIR: 0-1
├── Purpose: Functional overreach

Week 6: Deload
├── Volume: 50% of week 4
├── RIR: 5+
├── Purpose: Recover, supercompensate
```

## Deload Protocols

### Types of Deload

| Type | Volume | Intensity | Frequency | Best For |
|------|--------|-----------|-----------|----------|
| **Volume** | 50% | 100% | 100% | Accumulated fatigue |
| **Intensity** | 100% | 70% | 100% | CNS fatigue |
| **Full** | 50% | 70% | 50% | After intense blocks |
| **Active Rest** | 0% | 0% | 0% | After competition |

### When to Deload

**Scheduled (proactive):**
- Every 4-6 weeks during hypertrophy phases
- Every 3-4 weeks during strength phases
- After any peaking block

**Reactive (based on symptoms):**
- Performance drop 2+ consecutive sessions
- Chronic soreness not resolving
- Sleep disturbances
- Mood/motivation changes
- Elevated resting heart rate
- Decreased grip strength

### Sample Deload Week

**If normal week is:**
- Squat 4×8 @ 140kg
- Bench 4×10 @ 100kg
- Row 4×10 @ 90kg

**Volume deload (50% sets):**
- Squat 2×8 @ 140kg
- Bench 2×10 @ 100kg
- Row 2×10 @ 90kg

**Full deload (50% volume, 70% intensity):**
- Squat 2×8 @ 100kg
- Bench 2×10 @ 70kg
- Row 2×10 @ 65kg

## Macrocycle Planning

### Annual Structure for Hypertrophy

```
Jan-Mar: Hypertrophy Block 1
├── Focus: Overall mass
├── Volume: Progressive increase
└── Caloric surplus

Apr-May: Strength Block
├── Focus: Convert gains to strength
├── Moderate volume
└── Maintenance calories

Jun-Aug: Hypertrophy Block 2
├── Focus: Weak points
├── Specialization
└── Moderate surplus

Sep-Oct: Cut/Definition (if competing)
├── Reduce volume 30-40%
├── Maintain intensity
└── Caloric deficit

Nov-Dec: Recovery/Transition
├── Lower intensity training
├── Active recovery
└── Maintenance
```

### For Non-Competitors

```
Repeating 6-week mesocycles:
├── 4-5 weeks progressive overload
├── 1 week deload
├── Repeat with new exercise variations
└── Take 1 full week off every 12-16 weeks
```

## Volume Progression Within Mesocycle

### Adding Volume Strategy

**Option 1: Add sets each week**
```
Week 1: 10 sets chest
Week 2: 12 sets chest (+2)
Week 3: 14 sets chest (+2)
Week 4: 16 sets chest (+2)
Week 5: 8 sets chest (deload)
```

**Option 2: Add reps then reset**
```
Week 1: 3×8 (24 reps)
Week 2: 3×9 (27 reps)
Week 3: 3×10 (30 reps)
Week 4: 3×12 (36 reps)
Week 5: 4×8 (32 reps, +weight)
```

**Option 3: Add exercises**
```
Week 1: 2 exercises × 3 sets
Week 2: 2 exercises × 3 sets + 1 exercise × 2 sets
Week 3: 3 exercises × 3 sets
Week 4: 3 exercises × 3 sets + drop set finisher
```

## Periodization for Different Goals

### Pure Hypertrophy Focus

- Longer accumulation phases (5-6 weeks)
- Moderate intensities (65-80% primarily)
- Higher rep ranges (8-15 main work)
- More isolation work
- Frequent deloads for recovery

### Strength-Hypertrophy Balance

- Shorter phases (3-4 weeks)
- Mix of intensities in same week (DUP)
- Rep range variety (3-15)
- Focus on compounds
- Strategic heavy singles/doubles

### Bodybuilding Competition Prep

```
Off-season (6-9 months):
├── Mass building phases
├── Progressive volume increase
└── Caloric surplus

Contest Prep (16-24 weeks):
├── Maintain volume initially
├── Reduce volume as deficit deepens
├── Maintain intensity (heavy weights)
├── Add cardio progressively
└── Peak week protocols
```

## Tracking and Adjustments

### What to Track

1. **Volume:** Sets per muscle per week
2. **Intensity:** Weight used, RPE/RIR
3. **Progression:** Reps achieved, weight increases
4. **Recovery:** Sleep, stress, soreness
5. **Body composition:** Weight, measurements

### When to Adjust Program

| Signal | Action |
|--------|--------|
| All sets at top of rep range | Increase weight 2.5-5% |
| Can't hit minimum reps | Reduce weight 5-10% |
| Stagnant 2+ weeks | Add 1-2 sets or change exercise |
| Excessive fatigue | Take deload |
| Joint pain | Modify exercise selection |
| Motivation dropping | Introduce variation |

### Red Flags Requiring Deload

- Performance down 3+ sessions
- Sleep quality degraded
- Appetite significantly reduced
- Resting HR elevated 10+ bpm
- Constant muscle soreness
- Mood disturbances
