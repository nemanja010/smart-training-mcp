## Tier Classification System

| Tier | Evidence Level | Recommendation |
|------|----------------|----------------|
| **Tier 1** | Strong (multiple meta-analyses) | Recommended |
| **Tier 2** | Solid (RCTs, consistent data) | Consider |
| **Tier 3** | Limited/Mixed | Optional |
| **Not Recommended** | Insufficient/Negative | Avoid |

## Tier 1 - Strongly Recommended

### Creatine Monohydrate

**The most researched and effective ergogenic supplement**

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Dose | 3-5 g/day (no loading required) |
| Timing | Any time, consistency matters |
| Benefits | +5-10% strength, +1-2 kg LBM |
| Safety | Extensively studied, very safe |
| Cost | ~$0.05/day |

**Mechanism:**
- Increases phosphocreatine stores
- Enhanced ATP regeneration
- Cell volumization
- May enhance satellite cell proliferation

**Who benefits most:**
- Responds better in those with lower baseline creatine
- Non-meat eaters often respond better
- ~30% are "non-responders"

**Loading protocol (optional):**
- 20g/day (4x5g) for 5-7 days
- Then 3-5g/day maintenance
- Faster saturation but not necessary

---

### Caffeine

**Acute performance enhancer**

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Dose | 3-6 mg/kg body weight |
| Timing | 30-60 min pre-workout |
| Benefits | +3-7% performance |
| Tolerance | Develops over time |
| Half-life | 4-6 hours |

**Example dosing (80kg):**
- Moderate: 240mg (3 mg/kg)
- Standard: 320-400mg (4-5 mg/kg)
- High: 480mg (6 mg/kg)

**Best practices:**
- Cycle off periodically (1-2 weeks every 8-12 weeks)
- Avoid after 2-3 PM (sleep impact)
- Start lower if caffeine-naive

**Forms:**
- Caffeine anhydrous (pills) - most studied
- Coffee (~95mg/cup) - additional compounds
- Pre-workouts (varies widely)

### Protein Powder

**Convenience supplement for hitting protein targets**

For detailed protein requirements, timing, and DIAAS rankings, see [nutrition.md](nutrition.md#protein-requirements).

| Type | Speed | Best Use |
|------|-------|----------|
| Whey Isolate | Fast | Post-workout, general |
| Whey Concentrate | Fast | Cost-effective option |
| Casein | Slow | Before bed |
| Blend | Mixed | Any time |
| Plant (pea+rice) | Medium | Vegans |

**When to use:**
- Not hitting protein goals from food
- Convenience (travel, busy schedule)
- Post-workout when whole food not practical
- Before bed (casein)

**Not a replacement for whole foods**

## Tier 2 - Consider Using

### Beta-Alanine

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Dose | 3.2-6.4 g/day |
| Timing | Split doses (reduces tingling) |
| Benefits | +2-3% endurance (60-240s efforts) |
| Side effect | Paresthesia (harmless tingling) |
| Saturation | 4+ weeks to full effect |

**Best for:**
- High-rep sets (15-30+ reps)
- Supersets, drop sets
- Conditioning work
- CrossFit-style training

**Mechanism:** Increases muscle carnosine → buffering H+ ions

---

### Citrulline (L-Citrulline or Citrulline Malate)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Dose | 6-8g L-Citrulline or 8-10g Citrulline Malate |
| Timing | 30-60 min pre-workout |
| Benefits | Enhanced "pump", possible +3-5% reps |
| Safety | Well tolerated |

**Mechanism:**
- Increases arginine → nitric oxide
- Vasodilation, blood flow
- May reduce fatigue perception

---

### Essential Amino Acids (EAA)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Dose | 10-15g |
| Timing | Intra-workout or between meals |
| Benefits | Maintains MPS during fasted/extended training |

**When useful:**
- Training fasted
- Very long sessions (>90 min)
- Between meals for elderly/anabolic resistance
- Cutting phase

**Not necessary if:** Eating protein-rich meals around training

---

### Vitamin D3

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Dose | 2000-5000 IU/day |
| Test first | Optimal blood level: 40-60 ng/ml |
| Benefits | Immune, muscle function, testosterone |

**Who needs it:**
- Those with limited sun exposure
- Northern latitudes (October-April)
- Darker skin tones
- Indoor workers

**Take with fat for absorption**

---

### Omega-3 (Fish Oil)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Dose | 2-3g EPA+DHA combined |
| Benefits | Anti-inflammatory, may enhance MPS |
| Quality | Look for third-party tested brands |

**Note:** Food (fatty fish 2-3x/week) is preferable

## Tier 3 - Limited Evidence

### Ashwagandha (KSM-66)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Dose | 300-600 mg/day |
| Timing | Morning or before bed |
| Possible benefits | Stress reduction, cortisol modulation, recovery |

**Research:** Some studies show strength/muscle benefits, but not consistent across all trials.

---

### HMB (β-Hydroxy β-Methylbutyrate)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Dose | 3g/day |
| Best for | Beginners, elderly, during caloric deficit |

**Note:** Limited benefit for trained individuals in caloric surplus

---

### Betaine (Trimethylglycine)

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Dose | 2.5g/day |
| Possible benefits | Minor strength improvement |

**Research:** Mixed results, not consistently effective

## NOT Recommended

### Turkesterone

**Status:** Heavily marketed, insufficient evidence

**Problems:**
- No quality human RCTs
- Bioavailability concerns
- Purity issues with many products
- Cost vs. benefit very poor

**Verdict:** Save your money

---

### Ecdysterone

**Status:** Conflicting data

**Problems:**
- Some positive rodent data, human data inconsistent
- Bioavailability issues
- Quality control concerns

**Verdict:** Insufficient evidence

---

### "Testosterone Boosters" (Tribulus, D-AA, etc.)

**Status:** Generally ineffective

| Product | Evidence |
|---------|----------|
| Tribulus terrestris | No effect on testosterone or muscle |
| D-Aspartic Acid | Temporary T increase, no muscle effect |
| Fenugreek | May slightly increase T, no muscle benefit |
| ZMA | Only helps if zinc deficient |

**Verdict:** None reliably increase muscle growth

---

### BCAAs (Branched-Chain Amino Acids)

**Status:** Obsolete for most situations

**Why not recommended:**
- EAAs are superior (contain all essential amino acids)
- Whole protein sources are even better
- BCAAs alone may actually compete with other amino acids

**Exception:** Could use if you specifically need leucine-heavy trigger (rare)

## Safety Notes

### Interactions
- Caffeine + ephedrine: Avoid (cardiovascular risk)
- High-dose fish oil + blood thinners: Consult doctor
- Creatine + nephrotoxic drugs: Monitor

### Testing
- Athletes: Use only third-party tested products (Informed Sport, NSF)
- Common contaminants: Stimulants, anabolic agents

### Quality Brands (Examples)
- Thorne
- NOW Foods
- Nutricost
- MyProtein
- Optimum Nutrition

Always verify third-party testing for your specific needs.

## Supplement Interactions and Timing

### Positive Interactions

| Combination | Benefit | Timing |
|-------------|---------|--------|
| Creatine + Carbs | Enhanced uptake | Post-workout with meal |
| Caffeine + Citrulline | Synergistic performance | 30-60 min pre-workout |
| Vitamin D + Fat | Better absorption | With fatty meal |
| Protein + Leucine | MPS maximization | Already present in whey |
| Beta-alanine + Creatine | Complementary mechanisms | Any time, daily |

### Negative Interactions / Timing Conflicts

| Avoid Combining | Reason | Solution |
|-----------------|--------|----------|
| Caffeine + Creatine (high dose) | May reduce creatine uptake (contested) | Take creatine separately |
| Calcium + Iron | Calcium inhibits iron absorption | Take 2+ hours apart |
| Caffeine + Sleep supplements | Opposing effects | 6+ hours apart |
| Zinc + Copper (high doses) | Competition for absorption | Different meals |
| Fiber + Fat-soluble vitamins | Reduced absorption | Take vitamins with low-fiber meals |

### Daily Timing Protocol

**Morning (with breakfast):**
- Vitamin D (with fat)
- Fish oil
- Multivitamin

**Pre-workout (30-60 min before):**
- Caffeine
- Citrulline
- Beta-alanine (or split through day)

**Post-workout (with meal):**
- Protein
- Creatine
- Carbs

**Before bed:**
- Casein protein
- Magnesium
- ZMA (if using)

### What NOT to Take Together

1. **Caffeine + Melatonin** - Counterproductive
2. **Multiple stimulants** - Cardiovascular risk
3. **Iron + Dairy** - Blocks absorption
4. **Fat-soluble vitamins + Fat blockers** - Reduced absorption

## Medical Contraindications

### Who Should Consult a Doctor First

| Condition | Supplements to Avoid/Monitor | Reason |
|-----------|------------------------------|--------|
| **Kidney disease** | Creatine (high doses), high protein | Additional renal load |
| **Liver disease** | Most supplements | Impaired metabolism |
| **Heart conditions** | Caffeine, stimulants | Cardiovascular stress |
| **Hypertension** | Caffeine, high-sodium pre-workouts | Blood pressure elevation |
| **Diabetes** | Creatine (monitor glucose) | May affect insulin sensitivity |
| **Pregnancy/Nursing** | Most supplements | Insufficient safety data |
| **Under 18** | All ergogenic aids | Developmental considerations |

### Medication Interactions

| Medication Class | Supplement Interaction |
|------------------|------------------------|
| **Blood thinners (Warfarin)** | Fish oil, vitamin E - increased bleeding risk |
| **Blood pressure meds** | Caffeine - may counteract effects |
| **Diabetes medications** | Creatine - monitor blood glucose closely |
| **Antidepressants (MAOIs)** | Caffeine, tyramine-containing supplements |
| **Stimulant medications** | Caffeine - additive effects, avoid |
| **Thyroid medications** | Take supplements 4+ hours apart |

### General Safety Rules

1. **Start low, go slow** - Begin with half recommended dose
2. **One at a time** - Introduce new supplements individually (2 weeks apart)
3. **Track reactions** - Note any side effects
4. **Cycle stimulants** - 8-12 weeks on, 2 weeks off for caffeine
5. **Medical clearance** - If any chronic condition, consult doctor first

### Red Flags - Stop Immediately

- Chest pain or palpitations
- Severe headaches
- Difficulty breathing
- Allergic reactions (rash, swelling)
- Severe GI distress
- Dizziness or fainting
