## Meal Prep Guidelines

### Weekly Prep Strategy

**Sunday Prep Session (2-3 hours):**

| Task | Time | Output |
|------|------|--------|
| Cook protein batch 1 | 45 min | 1.5kg chicken breast |
| Cook protein batch 2 | 45 min | 1kg lean beef mince |
| Cook carb batch 1 | 30 min | 1.5kg rice |
| Cook carb batch 2 | 30 min | 1kg sweet potato |
| Prep vegetables | 20 min | Chopped for 4-5 days |
| Boil eggs | 15 min | 12-18 eggs |
| Portion and store | 15 min | - |

---

### Batch Cooking Tips

#### Protein Prep

| Protein | Cooking Method | Batch Size | Storage | Reheat |
|---------|---------------|------------|---------|--------|
| Chicken breast | Oven 180°C 25min | 1.5-2kg | 4 days fridge | Microwave 2min |
| Ground beef | Pan fry, drain fat | 1-1.5kg | 4 days fridge | Pan or microwave |
| Salmon | Oven 200°C 15min | 600g | 3 days fridge | Oven 5min (best fresh) |
| Eggs (hard boiled) | Boil 10min | 12-18 | 7 days fridge | Cold or room temp |
| Turkey mince | Pan fry | 1kg | 4 days fridge | Pan or microwave |

#### Carb Prep

| Carb | Method | Batch Size | Storage |
|------|--------|------------|---------|
| Rice | Rice cooker or pot | 1-1.5kg dry | 5 days fridge, 3 months freezer |
| Sweet potato | Oven 200°C 45min | 1.5kg | 5 days fridge |
| Quinoa | Boil 15min | 500g dry | 5 days fridge |
| Oats | Overnight prep | 5 portions | 5 days fridge |
| Pasta | Boil, rinse, oil | 500g dry | 4 days fridge |

---

### Container System

**Recommended Setup:**

| Container Type | Quantity | Use |
|----------------|----------|-----|
| Large (1L) | 10-12 | Complete meals |
| Medium (500ml) | 6-8 | Snacks, sides |
| Small (250ml) | 6 | Sauces, nuts, portions |
| Shaker bottles | 2-3 | Protein shakes |

**Labeling System:**
- Day of week
- Meal number (1-6)
- Macros (optional but helpful)
- Use masking tape and marker

**Storage Guidelines:**
- Fresh cooked protein: 3-4 days fridge
- Cooked rice: 5 days fridge (cool quickly!)
- Cooked vegetables: 4-5 days fridge
- Freezer meals: 2-3 months

---

### Grab-and-Go Meal Assembly

**Morning routine (5 minutes):**
1. Grab pre-portioned containers from fridge
2. Add fresh items (fruit, greens)
3. Pack shaker with dry protein powder
4. Fill water bottle

---

### Money-Saving Tips

1. **Buy in bulk** - warehouse stores for protein, rice, oats
2. **Frozen vegetables** - equally nutritious, much cheaper
3. **Whole chicken** - break down yourself, use bones for broth
4. **Store brands** - often identical quality
5. **Seasonal produce** - cheaper and fresher
6. **Meal prep** - reduces waste and impulse eating
7. **Generic whey** - same quality as premium brands
8. **Eggs over meat** - some meals, 6 eggs = 36g protein for ~$1

## Substitution Guide

### Common Food Substitutions

#### Protein Swaps

| Original | Substitute | Notes |
|----------|------------|-------|
| Chicken breast | Turkey breast, pork tenderloin | Similar macros |
| Salmon | Mackerel, trout, sardines | All fatty fish |
| Beef steak | Lean ground beef, bison | Cost savings |
| Whey protein | Egg white protein, casein | Different digestion |
| Greek yogurt | Skyr, quark, cottage cheese | Texture differs |
| Eggs | Egg whites + whole eggs mix | Lower fat option |

#### Carb Swaps

| Original | Substitute | Notes |
|----------|------------|-------|
| White rice | Brown rice, quinoa, bulgur | More fiber |
| Oatmeal | Cream of rice, cream of wheat | Faster digesting |
| Sweet potato | Regular potato, butternut squash | Similar carbs |
| Pasta | Rice noodles, zucchini noodles | Gluten-free options |
| Bread | Rice cakes, corn tortillas | Lower calorie |
| Banana | Dates, mango, berries | Variety |

#### Fat Swaps

| Original | Substitute | Notes |
|----------|------------|-------|
| Olive oil | Avocado oil, coconut oil | Different smoke points |
| Almonds | Walnuts, cashews, macadamia | Vary omega profile |
| Peanut butter | Almond butter, sunflower butter | Nut-free option |
| Avocado | Olives, nuts | Different texture |

## Quick Reference Cards

### Pre-Workout Meal Formula
```
1-2 hours before:
- 0.3-0.4g/kg protein (30-40g for 80kg)
- 0.5-1g/kg carbs (50-80g for 80kg)
- Low fat (digestion speed)
- Low fiber (avoid GI issues)
```

### Post-Workout Meal Formula
```
Within 2 hours:
- 0.4-0.5g/kg protein (40-50g for 80kg)
- 0.5-1g/kg carbs (50-80g for 80kg)
- Moderate fat OK
- Whole foods or shake
```

### Before Bed Formula
```
- 30-40g slow-digesting protein (casein, cottage cheese)
- Low carbs (unless post-evening training)
- Moderate fat for satiety
- Avoid large meals (sleep quality)
```
