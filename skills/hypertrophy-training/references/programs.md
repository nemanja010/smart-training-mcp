## Program Selection Guide

| Experience | Recommended Split | Sessions/Week | Volume |
|------------|-------------------|---------------|--------|
| Beginner (0-2 yrs) | Full Body | 3 | 10-12 sets/muscle |
| Intermediate (2-5 yrs) | Upper/Lower or PPL | 4-5 | 12-20 sets/muscle |
| Advanced (5+ yrs) | PPL, PHAT, or specialized | 5-6 | 16-26+ sets/muscle |

## Specialization: Weak Point Program

### Example: Lagging Chest (4-week block)

**Week Structure:**
- Monday: Chest Specialization
- Tuesday: Pull
- Wednesday: Legs
- Thursday: Push (maintenance) + Chest top-off
- Friday: Pull
- Saturday: Legs (light) + Chest finisher

### Monday - Chest Specialization
| Exercise | Sets × Reps | Notes |
|----------|-------------|-------|
| Incline Barbell Bench | 4 × 6-8 | Heavy, focus |
| Flat DB Press | 4 × 10-12 | Stretch at bottom |
| Low Cable Fly | 4 × 12-15 | Lengthened emphasis |
| Dips (chest focus) | 3 × 10-12 | Lean forward |
| Push-ups | 2 × AMRAP | Burnout |

### Thursday - Chest Top-off
| Exercise | Sets × Reps |
|----------|-------------|
| Incline DB Press | 3 × 10-12 |
| Machine Fly | 3 × 15-20 |
| (Plus shoulders and triceps maintenance) |

### Volume Distribution
| Muscle | Before | During Specialization |
|--------|--------|----------------------|
| Chest | 12 sets | 20+ sets |
| Other muscles | Normal | -20-30% |

### Volume Landmarks per Muscle

- Recovery between sessions
- Performance maintenance
- Pump and connection quality
- Soreness levels

## Bodybuilding Competition Prep

### Timeline Overview (16-24 weeks out)

| Phase | Weeks Out | Focus | Volume | Calories |
|-------|-----------|-------|--------|----------|
| **Diet Start** | 24-20 | Establish deficit | 100% | -300 to -500 |
| **Fat Loss** | 20-12 | Steady progress | 90-100% | -500 to -750 |
| **Intensification** | 12-8 | Maintain muscle | 80-90% | -500 to -750 |
| **Sharpening** | 8-4 | Detail work | 70-80% | Adjust as needed |
| **Peak Week** | 1 | Water/carb manipulation | 50-60% | Variable |

### Training Adjustments During Prep

**Weeks 24-12:**
- Maintain training intensity (heavy weights)
- Volume can stay high initially
- Add cardio progressively (start with 20min LISS 3x/week)

**Weeks 12-4:**
- Reduce volume by 20-40% (not intensity)
- Focus on muscle preservation
- Increase cardio as needed (up to 45-60min daily)
- Prioritize compound movements

**Peak Week:**
- Light pump workouts only
- Focus on posing practice
- No heavy training 3-4 days before show

### Peak Week Protocol (Example)

| Day | Carbs | Water | Sodium | Training |
|-----|-------|-------|--------|----------|
| Sun (-7) | Normal | Normal | Normal | Light full body |
| Mon (-6) | Low (50g) | 6-8L | Normal | Depletion workout |
| Tue (-5) | Low (50g) | 6-8L | Normal | Depletion workout |
| Wed (-4) | Low (50g) | 4-6L | Reduce | Light pump |
| Thu (-3) | Carb load start | 2-3L | Low | Rest |
| Fri (-2) | Carb load | 1-2L | Low | Light posing |
| Sat (-1) | Moderate | Sip | Low | Show day |

**Warning:** Peak week is highly individual. Practice during prep to find what works for you. Many competitors look their best 1-2 days after the show due to mistimed peaks.

### Cardio During Prep

**Preferred modalities:**
- Walking (lowest interference)
- Cycling (low impact)
- Stairmaster (glute activation bonus)

**Avoid:**
- Running (high interference, catabolic)
- HIIT (preserves less muscle than LISS in deficit)

**Progressive approach:**
```
Week 24: 3x20min LISS
Week 20: 4x25min LISS
Week 16: 5x30min LISS
Week 12: 6x35min LISS
Week 8: 6x45min LISS (or 2x daily shorter)
Week 4: Adjust based on condition
```

### Posing Practice

- Start 16+ weeks out
- Daily practice (15-30 min)
- Video yourself weekly
- Practice in competition conditions (lights, time limits)
- Mandatory poses + transitions

### Mental Preparation

- Prep is mentally challenging - expect mood fluctuations
- Have support system (coach, training partner, family)
- Set process goals, not just outcome goals
- Plan post-show recovery strategy in advance

## Program Notes

### When to Change Programs
- Progress stalled 4+ weeks
- Boredom affecting effort
- Goals changed
- Life circumstances changed

### What to Keep Consistent
- Core movement patterns
- Progressive overload tracking
- RIR/RPE system
- Deload schedule

### Common Mistakes
1. Program hopping every 2-3 weeks
2. Not tracking progression
3. Ignoring fatigue signals
4. Neglecting weak points
5. Volume too high too soon
