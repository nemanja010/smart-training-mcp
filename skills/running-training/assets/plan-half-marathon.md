# Half Marathon Training Plan

## How to use

1. Establish VDOT from recent 5K or 10K (`scripts/vdot.py`)
2. All easy runs at E pace — strictly conversational
3. Two quality sessions per week; all others easy
4. Long run pacing: E pace for first 12 weeks; marathon-pace sections appear in final 6 weeks

**Prerequisite:** Comfortable running 55+ km/week, completed a 10K race.

---

## Physiological priorities for the half marathon

1. **Lactate threshold (LT2)** — the dominant limiter; half marathon is run at ~88–92% VO2max, just below threshold
2. **Aerobic base** — capacity to run comfortably for 60–90+ minutes
3. **VO2max** — sets the ceiling that LT % of VO2max can reach
4. **Running economy** — contributes more at faster paces

---

## 14-Week Half Marathon Plan

### Phase overview

| Phase | Weeks | Volume | Key work |
|-------|-------|--------|---------|
| Base | 1–4 | 55–70 km/wk | E miles, long runs, strides |
| Build 1 | 5–8 | 65–80 km/wk | Threshold + VO2max |
| Build 2 | 9–12 | 70–85 km/wk | Extended threshold, long runs with HM pace |
| Peak + Taper | 13–14 | Taper | Sharpen; race |

### Weekly structure

```
Mon: Rest or very easy 30 min
Tue: Quality 1 (Q1)
Wed: Easy 45–55 min
Thu: Easy 50–60 min
Fri: Quality 2 (Q2) or medium-long easy run
Sat: Easy 40–50 min + strides
Sun: Long run
```

### Week-by-week plan

#### Base phase

**Week 1**
- Q1: 3 × 10 min @ T pace, 90 sec rest | WU+CD: 25 min
- Q2: 40 min easy + 8 strides
- Long: 80 min E

**Week 2**
- Q1: 4 × 8 min @ T pace, 90 sec rest
- Q2: 5 × 1000m @ I pace, 3 min jog
- Long: 85 min E

**Week 3**
- Q1: 20 min sustained tempo @ T pace
- Q2: 4 × 1200m @ I pace, 3 min jog
- Long: 90 min E

**Week 4 (Recovery)**
- No quality sessions; all easy
- Long: 65 min E

---

#### Build 1

**Week 5**
- Q1: 4 × 10 min @ T pace, 2 min rest
- Q2: 5 × 1000m @ I pace, 3 min jog
- Long: 90 min E

**Week 6**
- Q1: 25 min sustained tempo
- Q2: 4 × 1200m @ I pace, 3 min jog
- Long: 95 min E

**Week 7**
- Q1: 5 × 8 min @ T pace, 90 sec rest
- Q2: 3 × 1600m @ I pace, 4 min jog
- Long: 100 min E (optional: last 15 min at HM goal pace)

**Week 8 (Recovery)**
- Q1 only: 3 × 8 min @ T pace
- Long: 70 min E

---

#### Build 2

**Week 9**
- Q1: 3 × 12 min @ T pace, 2 min rest
- Q2: 6 × 1000m @ I pace, 3 min jog
- Long: 100 min E + last 20 min at HM pace

**Week 10**
- Q1: 30 min sustained tempo
- Q2: 4 × 1200m @ I pace, 3 min jog + 8 strides
- Long: 110 min E + last 20–25 min at HM pace

**Week 11**
- Q1: 4 × 10 min @ T pace, 90 sec rest
- Q2: 10 min E + 25 min HM pace + 10 min E (progression)
- Long: 105 min E + last 25 min at HM pace

**Week 12 (Light recovery)**
- Q1: 2 × 15 min @ T pace, 2 min rest
- Long: 80 min E

---

#### Peak + Taper

**Week 13 (Taper begins)**
- Q1: 3 × 8 min @ T pace, 90 sec rest
- Q2: 4 × 800m @ I pace + 6 strides
- Long: 70 min E (no race-pace work this week)
- Total volume: 60% of peak week

**Week 14 (Race week)**
- Mon: 30 min easy
- Tue: 20 min easy + 4 × 200m @ I pace + 6 strides
- Wed: 25 min easy
- Thu: 20 min easy + 4 strides
- Fri: Rest
- Sat: 20 min very easy + 4 strides (optional pre-race)
- **Sun: Half marathon race**

## Race execution

### Pacing strategy

**Goal pace:** Add 10–15 sec/km to your best 10K average pace.

**Mile-by-mile:**
- **Km 1–2:** 8–10 sec/km conservative. Resist the crowd pace.
- **Km 3–10:** Settle at goal pace. This is the bulk of the race.
- **Km 10–18:** Maintain; this is where under-fueled and over-paced runners fade.
- **Km 18–21.1:** Give everything. Commit.

### HR targets
- Km 1–3: 85–88% LTHR
- Km 3–17: 88–93% LTHR
- Km 17–finish: up to 95–98% LTHR

### Nutrition
- Races >90 min: 1–2 gels during race
- Take water at every aid station
- Don't wait to feel thirsty or hungry

### Splits mistake
The single most common half marathon error: running the first 5 km at 10K effort. Creates a glycogen and lactate debt that costs 10 minutes in the final 5 km.

## Expected improvements by baseline

| Current HM time | After 14 weeks of consistent training |
|----------------|---------------------------------------|
| 2:30–2:00 | −8 to −15 min |
| 2:00–1:45 | −5 to −10 min |
| 1:45–1:30 | −3 to −6 min |
| 1:30–1:20 | −2 to −4 min |

Improvements are larger when base fitness was not optimized. Gains diminish with experience.
