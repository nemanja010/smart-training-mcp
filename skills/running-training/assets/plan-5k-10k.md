# 5K / 10K Training Plans

## How to use these plans

1. Find your current VDOT using `scripts/vdot.py --distance [your distance] --time [your time]`
2. Use that VDOT to find your training paces in `references/zones-and-testing.md`
3. Run all easy/E-pace sessions conservatively — slower is better than faster
4. Quality sessions are the 2 key sessions per week (marked **Q**)
5. Adjust if life intervenes — skip an easy day, never skip a Q session

**Prerequisite:** Running consistently 4+ days/week for at least 3 months. Can run 30+ min continuously.

## 8-Week 5K Plan

**Target athlete:** Recreational runner with VDOT 38–55, running 35–55 km/week.
**Goal:** Improve 5K time 60–120 seconds.
**Priority workouts:** VO2max intervals + Repetitions + Speed.

### Weekly structure
```
Mon: Rest or cross-train
Tue: Quality 1 (Q1) — VO2max intervals or repetitions
Wed: Easy 35–45 min + strides
Thu: Easy 45–50 min
Fri: Quality 2 (Q2) — tempo or shorter intervals
Sat: Easy 40–50 min
Sun: Long run 70–80 min (E pace)
```

### Plan

| Week | Q1 | Q2 | Long run | Weekly km (approx) |
|------|----|----|----------|-------------------|
| 1 | 5×1000m @ I pace, 3 min jog | 6×200m @ R pace, full recovery | 70 min E | 38–45 km |
| 2 | 6×800m @ I pace, 2 min jog | 8×200m @ R pace | 75 min E | 40–48 km |
| 3 | 4×1200m @ I pace, 3 min jog | 6×400m @ R pace, 3 min walk | 75 min E | 42–50 km |
| 4 | **Recovery week** — all easy; short strides only | | 60 min E | 30–35 km |
| 5 | 3×1600m @ I pace, 4 min jog | 8×400m @ R pace, 2 min walk | 80 min E | 42–50 km |
| 6 | 5×1200m @ I pace, 3 min jog | 6×200m + 3×400m @ R pace | 80 min E | 44–52 km |
| 7 | 6×1000m @ I pace, 3 min jog | 10×200m @ R pace | 70 min E | 40–48 km |
| 8 | **Taper** — Tue: 4×600m @ I pace; Fri: 6×200m R pace | | 50 min E | 28–32 km |
| Race week | Mon: easy; Wed: 4×200m @ R pace + strides | Rest Fri | **5K race** | 20 km |

### Notes
- All Q sessions include 10–15 min easy warmup and 10 min easy cooldown
- Week 4 and 8 are reduced recovery/taper — do not skip these
- Strides: 6–8 × 20 sec accelerations at end of easy runs, 2–3×/week

## 8-Week 10K Plan

**Target athlete:** VDOT 38–58, running 45–65 km/week.
**Goal:** Improve 10K time 2–4 minutes.
**Priority workouts:** Threshold + VO2max intervals.

### Weekly structure
```
Mon: Rest or 30 min very easy
Tue: Q1 — VO2max intervals
Wed: Easy 45–55 min
Thu: Q2 — Threshold
Fri: Easy 40–50 min
Sat: Easy 45–50 min + strides
Sun: Long run 80–90 min
```

### Plan

| Week | Q1 | Q2 | Long run | Weekly km |

| 1 | 5×1000m @ I pace, 3 min | 2×15 min @ T pace, 2 min rest | 80 min E | 48–58 km |
| 2 | 4×1200m @ I pace, 3 min | 20 min sustained tempo @ T | 85 min E | 50–60 km |
| 3 | 6×800m @ I pace, 2 min | 3×12 min @ T pace, 2 min rest | 85 min E | 52–62 km |
| 4 | **Recovery** — all easy; strides only | | 65 min E | 35–40 km |
| 5 | 5×1200m @ I pace, 3 min | 25 min sustained tempo | 85 min E | 52–62 km |
| 6 | 4×1600m @ I pace, 4 min | 4×8 min @ T, 90 sec rest | 85 min E | 54–64 km |

## 12-Week 10K Plan (more gradual)

For runners building toward their first sub-60 or sub-50 10K with less base.

| Phase | Weeks | Focus |
|-------|-------|-------|
| Base | 1–4 | Mileage + strides + easy hills |
| Build | 5–10 | Threshold + VO2max |
| Peak/Taper | 11–12 | Sharpen + race |

### Base phase (Weeks 1–4)

```
Week 1:
  Tue: 40 min E + 6 strides
  Thu: 45 min E
  Sat: 55 min E
  Sun: 65 min E long

Week 2:
  Tue: 40 min E + 6 strides
  Thu: 45 min E + optional hill strides (6×30 sec uphill)
  Sat: 50 min E
  Sun: 70 min E long

Week 3:
  Tue: 4×800m @ I pace (first quality introduction), 2 min jog
  Thu: 20 min easy + 15 min @ T pace + 10 min easy
  Sat: 50 min E + strides
  Sun: 70 min E long

Week 4 (Recovery):
  All easy; 4 days running; longest 55 min
```

### Build phase (Weeks 5–10) — as per 8-week plan above

### Tune-up race week 11

Run a 5K at full effort 2–3 weeks before goal 10K. Use result to update VDOT and check pacing.

## Racing strategy

### 5K
- Km 1: 3–5 sec/km conservative (the pack goes out too fast; let them)
- Km 2–3: settle at goal pace; this is the hardest section mentally
- Km 4: hold on; if you have anything left start pushing
- Km 5: give everything

### 10K
- First 2 km: 5–8 sec/km conservative
- Km 2–8: consistent effort (not pace — effort adjusts for hills)
- Km 8–10: give everything
- Even-split or slight negative split is optimal

### Pacing notes
- Both 5K and 10K should feel "impossible to maintain" in the final 1–2 km
- If you had energy to spare at the finish, you ran too conservatively
- Heart rate will be at maximum (95–100% HRmax) in the final km of a well-executed 5K
