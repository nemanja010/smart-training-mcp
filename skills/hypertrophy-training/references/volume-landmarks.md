## What Are Volume Landmarks

> **Warning:** Volume landmarks (MEV/MAV/MRV) are practical heuristics from Renaissance Periodization, not precise scientific data. Values are approximate and **vary significantly between individuals** (up to ±50%).

| Abbreviation | Name | Definition |
|--------------|------|------------|
| **MEV** | Minimum Effective Volume | Lowest volume for any growth |
| **MAV** | Maximum Adaptive Volume | Optimal range for most people |
| **MRV** | Maximum Recoverable Volume | Maximum you can still recover from |

## Individual Factors

**Factors affecting individual values:**

| Factor | Lower volume need | Higher volume need |
|--------|-------------------|-------------------|
| Training history | Advanced (more efficient) | Beginners |
| Genetics | High responders | Low responders |
| Recovery capacity | Lower | Higher |
| Stress/sleep | High stress, poor sleep | Optimal |
| Age | Older (>40) | Younger |
| Nutrition | Deficit | Surplus |

---

## How to Find Your Values

1. Start at lower end of recommendations
2. Monitor progress for 4-6 weeks
3. If progress stalls → add 2 sets
4. If recovery is insufficient → remove 2 sets
